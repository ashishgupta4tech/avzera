  • Separation of Consents: Service consultation consent is kept separate from any marketing or promotional communication.

7. Data Security Safeguards & Breach Response Protocol (DPDP Act s.8(5), s.8(6))

To prevent personal data breaches, we maintain robust reasonable security safeguards:

  • Encryption & Transport Security: 100% of website traffic is encrypted in transit via HTTPS / TLS 1.3 with valid SSL certificates. Database files are encrypted at rest.
  • Access Control: Strict least-privilege role access, strong multi-character credentials, and two-factor authentication (2FA).
  • Logging & Auditing: Secure access logs are preserved for at least one year as prescribed by the 7th Schedule of DPDP Rules 2025.
  • Written Data Breach Response Plan: In the event of any unauthorized access, leak, or breach of personal data:
    1. We will inform all affected Data Principals without undue delay with clear details of the incident and recommended mitigations.
    2. We will submit a formal, detailed breach report to the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) within 72 hours as prescribed by DPDP Rules.
    3. Cyber security incidents will be reported to CERT-In within 6 hours of detection, in compliance with CERT-In Directions (April 2022).

8. Your Statutory Rights Under the DPDP Act 2023 (Sections 11–14)

As a Data Principal, the law grants you the following enforceable rights:

1. Right to Access & Summary (s.11) Request a full copy and summary of all personal data held by us, along with processing identities.
2. Right to Correction & Erasure (s.12) Request immediate rectification of inaccurate data, completion of incomplete records, or permanent erasure.
3. Right of Grievance Redressal (s.13) Readily accessible grievance resolution directly with our designated officer within statutory deadlines.
4. Right to Nominate a Nominee (s.14) Nominate another individual to exercise your data rights in the event of death or incapacity.
