1. Milestone Structure & Refund Eligibility

Our service agreements operate on clearly defined milestone sprints to ensure complete transparency:

  • Pre-Staging Cancellation (Before Draft Delivery): If you request project cancellation before we deliver the initial staging preview (within the first 24 hours of kickoff), your 40% advance is refunded in full, minus any non-recoverable third-party domain/SSL registry charges.
  • 7-Day Staging Review Period: Once your private staging preview is delivered, you have a 7-day window to evaluate and test the deliverables. If the delivered work fundamentally fails to meet the documented scope and we are unable to rectify it within 3 revision cycles, you are under zero obligation to pay the 60% balance.
  • Final Sign-Off & Balance Payment: Once you approve the final delivery, authorize deployment to your live production domain, and settle the 60% balance, the project is deemed successfully completed. Due to the irrevocable transfer of source code, digital credentials, and creative assets, completed deliverables are non-refundable.

2. Transparent Pricing & Zero Hidden Charges

In compliance with Rule 4 of Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020:

  • Every project estimate provides a full break-up of charges in writing prior to any payment.
  • No automatic subscriptions, recurring hidden fees, or unwanted upsells are sneaked into your account.
  • Accepted payment modes: UPI (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm), Direct Bank Transfer (NEFT/RTGS), and verified payment gateways.

3. Third-Party Pass-Through Costs

Fees paid directly to third-party providers (e.g. custom domain name registrars, third-party SMS/WhatsApp gateway credit balances, or ad spend paid directly to Google/Meta) are non-refundable as they are directly disbursed to third-party platforms.
