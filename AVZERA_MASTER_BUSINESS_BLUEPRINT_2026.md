1. **The Google Maps Leak:** Over **73% of local high-intent customer calls** originate from the Google Maps 3-Pack (the top 3 listings shown when someone searches "dentist near me", "marble showroom Raipur", or "hotel in Bhilai"). ### AVZERA's Synchronized 3-Pillar Solution
