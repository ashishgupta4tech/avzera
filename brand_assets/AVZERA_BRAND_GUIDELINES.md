## 1. The Core Monogram: Origin to Mark

The AVZERA brand mark is derived directly from Founder Vikas Vivan's handwritten signature—a single, unbroken, continuous unistroke combining the letters **A**, **V**, and **Z**.

### The Geometry
- **Letter A**: The ascending triangle rising from the origin, crowned with a precision apex and anchored by a central horizontal crossbeam.
- **Letter V**: The descending inverted spike pointing toward technical rigor and zero-compromise precision.
- **Letter Z**: The upper horizontal beam, downward-left transition diagonal, and bottom runner beam ending in a floating terminal network node.

```
M 36 470 L 542 28 L 465 540 L 285 142 L 660 142 L 275 335 A 39.5 39.5 0 0 0 275 414 L 685 414
Terminal Node: Circle at (744, 414), radius 19
```

### The Interlacing (Over-and-Under Weave)
The mark features mathematical interlacing where crossing strands alternate in depth, creating a woven Celtic-knot dimensional ribbon:
1. **S3 over S1** (spine of A crosses over the start diagonal)
2. **S1 over S4** (start diagonal crosses over top horizontal bar)
3. **S4 over S2** (top horizontal bar crosses over right triangle leg)
4. **S2 over S5** (right triangle leg crosses over descending diagonal)
5. **S3 over S5** (spine of A crosses over descending diagonal)
6. **S3 over S6** (spine of A crosses over bottom runner)
7. **S6 over S2** (bottom runner crosses over right leg)

## 4. Typography Rules

- **Wordmark ("AVZERA")**: Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans`, weight `900 (Black)`, tracking `+12` to `+16` em.
- **Descriptor / Tagline**: Set in `JetBrains Mono`, weight `700 (Bold)`, tracking `+5` to `+6` em, uppercase only.
- **Minimum Clear Space**: A minimum margin equal to the height of the terminal dot ($19\text{px}$) must be maintained on all sides of the mark.
